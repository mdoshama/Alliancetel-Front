SMS Opt-In Consent

AllianceTel work schedule text messages · NAZRA ANWAR LLC, doing business as AllianceTel

This page shows how employees of NAZRA ANWAR LLC (AllianceTel) agree to receive work schedule text messages. Employees opt in themselves, online, inside the AllianceTel workforce app at atdirect.app. Managers and administrators cannot opt anyone in. Text messages are optional: consent is not a condition of employment, and schedules are always available in the app and by email.

How an employee opts in

  1. The employee signs in to the AllianceTel app with their own account. Their mobile number is on their employee record.
  2. They open their account menu (top right) and switch on By text message.
  3. The text message consent form opens, showing their mobile number, the consent language below, and links to the SMS Terms and Privacy Policy.
  4. They tick the unchecked I agree box. The Turn on text messages button stays disabled until they do.
  5. They tap Turn on text messages. The app records the date and time of consent and sends the confirmation text below.

The consent form in the app

Consent language shown to the employee

By turning on text messages, you agree to receive recurring automated work schedule texts from AllianceTel (NAZRA ANWAR LLC) at the mobile number on your account: your weekly schedule, schedule changes, day-off decisions and team announcements. Message frequency varies, usually 1 to 6 messages per week. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of employment.
