Delete your AllianceTel account

AllianceTel Enterprise · AllianceTel app for iPhone, Android and the web

In the app (fastest)

  1. Sign in to the AllianceTel app or at atdirect.app.
  2. Open My Profile (tap your name at the top right).
  3. Under Security, choose Delete my account, enter your password and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you're signed out on every device.

If you can't sign in

Email support@atdirect.app from the email address on your account (or include the phone number on your account) with the subject "Delete my AllianceTel account". We confirm it's you and delete the account within 7 days. You can also first reset your password with "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in page and delete it yourself.

What is deleted

  • Your name, employee code (NTID), email address, phone number and birth date.
  • Your password and sign-in, on every device.
  • The messages sent to you (email, WhatsApp, text) and the reasons you gave for days off.
  • Your messaging preferences and text-message consent.

What is kept

Past schedules, days off and time clock records are business records the company must keep for payroll, tax and legal purposes. They stay without your personal details, shown as "Deleted user". See our Privacy Policy.
