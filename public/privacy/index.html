Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AllianceTel Enterprise ("AllianceTel", "we", "us"), collects, uses and protects personal information in the AllianceTel workforce app at atdirect.app (the "App"), which our employees and managers use for work schedules, days off, end-of-day sales reports, performance and commission, and for schedule messages by email, WhatsApp and text message (SMS).

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, work email address, mobile phone number, employee code, role, area and home store.
  • Work records: schedules, shifts, day-off requests and decisions, end-of-day sales and close-out entries, performance figures, commission, fines and rewards.
  • Messaging preferences and consent: whether you chose to get updates by email, WhatsApp or text message, when you agreed, and the delivery status of messages we send you (for example "delivered" or "failed").
  • Sign-in and security data: sign-in times and failed sign-in attempts (including the IP address they came from), used to protect your account.

How we use your information

  • To run the App: build and publish schedules, handle day-off requests, record end-of-day sales, and work out performance and commission.
  • To send you the work messages you chose: your schedule, schedule changes, day-off decisions and team announcements, by email, WhatsApp or text message.
  • To keep accounts secure, prevent misuse, and meet legal and payroll obligations.

Text messages (SMS)

If you turn on text messages in the App, we use your mobile number only to send you work schedule messages from AllianceTel. We record that you agreed and when. You can stop at any time by replying STOP or turning text messages off in your account menu. See the SMS Terms in our Terms & Conditions.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Who we share information with

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run the App, and only so they can provide that service: our hosting provider (Railway), our email provider (Resend), and our messaging providers (Twilio and Meta/WhatsApp) to deliver the messages you chose. They may not use it for their own purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties, except the providers needed to deliver the messages. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep work records for as long as you work with us and afterwards as required for payroll, tax and legal purposes. Message records are kept so managers can see what was sent.

Security

Access is limited by role (employee, manager, administrator). Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, connections are encrypted (HTTPS), and repeated failed sign-ins lock the account temporarily.

Your choices

  • Turn email, WhatsApp or text message updates on or off at any time from your account menu.
  • Reply STOP to any text to stop texts, or HELP for help.
  • Ask your manager or contact us to see or correct your information.

Children

The App is for AllianceTel staff and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. We'll change the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the App.
